Why ambofit

Two apps can’t see
what one app can.

In Latin, ambo means both. Most people who lift track workouts in one app and calories in another, and neither knows what the other sees. ambofit puts both on one screen and reads them together.

Training and food, connected

The weekly check-in looks at both. If your lifts drop while protein runs low on a diet, ambofit tells you. A single-purpose app can’t.

Training

Know when to
go heavier.

Start today’s workout, tick off sets with one tap and let the rest timer run. When you hit your reps in every set, ambofit sets the weight for next time.

  • Target hit, weight up. After your last set, you see the result and next time’s weight, like 100 kg instead of 97.5 kg. Pro
  • Your plan shows what’s next. Hold, +5 kg or new, before you even start. Pro
  • Plans the way you train. Upper/lower or your own split, supersets and myo-reps.
  • Rest timer that keeps going. Time and next set on your Lock Screen, with a sound when it’s time, even with the app in the background.
  • Stuck? You’ll know. The check-in spots lifts that stopped moving and suggests a lighter week when several drop.

Questions

Is there a free version?

No. Every plan starts with 7 days free, so you can try everything first. If you don’t continue, everything you logged stays saved on your iPhone, and you can export it as CSV files from the subscription screen.

What’s the difference between Basic and Pro?

Pro adds auto-adjust weights after every exercise and AI photo logging with 10 photos a day. Everything else is in Basic, including the coached calorie goal with the weekly check-in and 3 AI photos to try.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your private iCloud, so it’s back on a new iPhone. There is no ambofit account and no copy of your workouts, meals or weight on our servers. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Logging workouts and foods, the food search and all insights work without a connection. Looking up a new barcode and AI photo need internet.

Which iPhones are supported?

Any iPhone with iOS 18 or later.

When can I get ambofit?

ambofit is in testing right now and coming soon to the App Store. Questions until then? Write to support@ambofit.com.
